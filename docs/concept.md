# Concept

> What if another camera captured the story?

## Premise

OFF CAMERA retells known fictional moments from cameras that would plausibly exist inside the story's world but were never part of the film's coverage. The audience already knows the scene. The new part is the point of view, and the visual grammar of whatever device recorded it.

## The Rule

> **Boring cameras witnessing extraordinary events.**

The camera doesn't know it is in a movie. It doesn't frame the hero, follow the action or cut away. It records what crosses its field of view, at its own resolution, with its own flaws.

## Selection Criteria

A concept moves forward only if it passes all four checks:

| Check | Question |
|-------|----------|
| **Plausible** | Would this device realistically be in this location? |
| **Constrained** | Is the framing dictated by the mount or the use, not by artistic choice? |
| **Uncovered** | Does it show the moment from somewhere the film never did? |
| **Recognizable** | Will viewers identify the moment within a few seconds, without a caption? |

## Story Arc

```
Normal → Weird → Recognition → Payoff
```

- **Normal:** the camera's ordinary reality, held long enough to establish it
- **Weird:** something enters that doesn't belong
- **Recognition:** the viewer realizes what they are watching
- **Payoff:** the moment plays out and ends on a beat worth rewatching

Target runtime is **10–18 seconds**.

## What This Project Is Not

- Not a recreation of the original shots
- Not parody, mashup or meme content
- Not presented as official, leaked or real footage
