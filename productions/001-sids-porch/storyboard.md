# Storyboard: Sid's Porch

Single continuous shot. Locked-off doorbell camera. No cuts.

| Time | Beat | Action | Notes |
|------|------|--------|-------|
| 0:00–0:03 | **Normal** | Clip starts on motion trigger. Empty porch, welcome mat, steps, a dim front yard. A leaf drifts across. | Establish the frame and the overlay. Nothing happens. |
| 0:03–0:06 | **Weird** | Something small moves at the bottom edge of the frame near the steps. Fisheye distortion makes it hard to read. | Keep it partial: shadows, a limb. |
| 0:06–0:10 | **Recognition** | A mutant toy pulls itself onto the porch into the IR light: Babyface on its spider legs. A second shape follows. | The moment the viewer clocks *Toy Story*. |
| 0:10–0:14 | **Payoff** | The toy stops, and the doll head slowly turns to look straight up into the camera. Hold. | End on the stare. Cut to black. |

## Frame Notes

- Fisheye lens, top of the frame slightly curved, porch railing bowing at the edges
- Mounted about 1.2 m high, angled down roughly 30°
- Welcome mat lower center, steps lower third, yard and street beyond
- Porch light off; IR illuminator gives a soft hotspot at the center
