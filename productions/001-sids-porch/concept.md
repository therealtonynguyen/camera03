**Status:** Pre-production

| | |
|---|---|

| **Runtime target** | 12–15 seconds |
| **Time of day** | Dusk into early night |

## Logline

A suburban doorbell camera records an empty porch at dusk until something small and wrong crawls up the steps.

## Why This Camera

A doorbell camera on a front door is about as ordinary as a camera gets. It is motion-triggered, mounted at a fixed height and angled down at the porch, which is exactly where the toys would cross. Its fisheye distortion and low-light IR mode make small, strange shapes harder to read, and that helps the reveal.

## Why This Moment

- Instantly recognizable characters in an iconic location
- One location, fixed framing, small subjects: manageable for current models
- The Normal → Weird → Recognition arc is built into the scene
- A clean test of the project's core premise
