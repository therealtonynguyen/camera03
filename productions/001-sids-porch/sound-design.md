# Sound Design: Sid's Porch

The device mic is small, mono and compressed.

## Layers

| Layer | Description | Timing |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Ambience | Suburban night: distant dog, crickets, faint traffic | Full clip |
| Device | Motion-trigger chime or click, low electrical hiss | 0:00 |
| Wind | Light mic buffeting | Intermittent |
| Toy movement | Metallic clicking and scraping on wooden steps | 0:03–0:10 |
| Stare | Ambience drops slightly; a faint servo whir as the head turns | 0:10–0:14 |
| Out | Hard cut to silence | 0:14 |

## Notes

- No music.
- Roll off the high and low end to sell the small speaker.
- Mono, slight clipping on the loudest transients.
