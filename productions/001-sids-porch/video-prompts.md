# Video Prompts: Sid's Porch

Motion only. Composition comes from the reference frame.

## Shot A: Approach and stare

**Input:** `image-prompts.md` v1 or v2

```
Static locked-off doorbell camera. A small mutant toy with a baby doll head
and spider-like metal legs climbs the porch steps from the bottom of frame,
skittering in short jerky bursts, legs clicking on the wood. It stops near
the welcome mat, then the doll head slowly tilts up and stares directly into
the camera. Low frame rate, slight stutter.
```

## Constraints

```
no camera movement, no pan, no zoom, no dolly
preserve fixed doorbell camera framing and fisheye distortion
preserve infrared black-and-white look
preserve compression artifacts and grain
no depth of field, no cinematic lighting, no color grading
timestamp overlay stays fixed
```
