# Image Prompts: Sid's Porch

Reference frames for image-to-video. Lock the camera here, not in the video prompt.

## v1: Empty porch (Normal)

```
Doorbell camera footage, extreme fisheye lens, mounted on a front door frame
about 1.2 meters high angled down at a suburban front porch at dusk. Worn
welcome mat lower center, wooden steps leading down to an overgrown front
yard, chain-link fence and a dim street beyond. Porch light off. Infrared
night mode, grainy black and white, soft IR hotspot in the center falling
off to dark edges. Heavy compression artifacts, low resolution, slight
barrel distortion bending the porch railings. Small white timestamp in the
top corner. Deep focus, no depth of field. Realistic security camera still.
```

## v2: Toy on porch (Recognition)

```
[v1 prompt] + A small mutant toy on the porch near the welcome mat: a bald
baby doll head mounted on a spidery metal Erector-set body, mid-crawl,
partially lit by the IR illuminator, slightly motion-blurred.
```
