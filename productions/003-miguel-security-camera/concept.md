# CAM 03 / 003: Coco, Miguel Security Camera

**Status:** Concept

| | |
|---|---|
| **Film** | Coco (2017) |
| **Moment** | Miguel crosses into the Land of the Dead after taking Ernesto's guitar |
| **Camera** | Cemetery security camera |
| **Runtime target** | 12–16 seconds |
| **Time of day** | Night, Día de Muertos |

## Logline

A cemetery security camera watches Miguel walk among the marigold-covered graves, and then he flickers, as if the camera can no longer quite see him.

## Why This Camera

A security camera overlooking a cemetery on the busiest night of the year is plausible. Its low frame rate and compression suit a supernatural moment: the camera can glitch, lose the subject or show something it can't explain.
