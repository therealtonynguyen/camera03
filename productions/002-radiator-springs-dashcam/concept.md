**Status:** Concept

| | |
|---|---|
| **Film** | Cars (2006) |
| **Moment** | Lightning McQueen and Doc Hudson race past on the desert road |

| **Runtime target** | 10–14 seconds |
| **Time of day** | Late afternoon, low sun |

## Logline

A dashcam on a quiet desert highway catches two race cars tearing past, one of them an old Hudson Hornet.

## Why This Camera

Dashcams run constantly and capture whatever happens on the road. The forward, vibrating, glare-washed view is instantly familiar, and two cars blowing past at speed is exactly what dashcam footage is known for.
